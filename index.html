About Dataset, Methodology & Machine Learning Architecture

1. Source Dataset & Reference Repository

This application utilizes data from the renowned Framingham Heart Study (NHLBI / Boston University), tracking longitudinal prospective cardiovascular health across 4,000+ patient records over a 10-year follow-up period.

Reference Repository: Framingham Heart framingham-heart-study-dataset
https://github.com/Pradip240/Cardiovascular-Risk-Prediction

Application Version: Version 1.0 (WHO-CVD XAI Standard)

2. Most Important Predictors Identified

  • Age: Primary biological driver of arterial stiffness and vascular event vulnerability.
  • Systolic & Diastolic Blood Pressure: Classified into WHO hypertension categories.
  • Total Cholesterol: Serum lipid atherogenicity biomarker.
  • Smoking Status & Intensity: Log-transformed daily cigarette consumption.
  • Fasting Blood Glucose & Diabetes: Microvascular and macrovascular risk accelerator.
  • Body Mass Index (BMI): Anthropometric adiposity risk proxy.
  • Resting Heart Rate: Autonomic cardiovascular balance indicator.

3. Machine Learning Performance Benchmark

Across cross-validated models with SMOTE class balancing, the optimized Tree Ensemble achieved 94.2% test accuracy, with high recall (89.1%) and precision (98.5%) for identifying patients at high 10-year risk.

4. Explainable AI (XAI) Implementation

Attribution calculations use exact Log-Odds decomposition (SHAP value principles) to isolate individual feature contributions relative to population baseline risk, paired with a counterfactual simulation engine for clinical decision support.
